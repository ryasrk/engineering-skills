# Topik 5: Laporan Harian (daily standup, daily status report, pelaporan proyek ke manajemen/klien)

Legenda sitasi (dipakai konsisten di seluruh catatan): **[STANDAR RESMI]** = standar/spesifikasi/regulasi yang diterbitkan badan otoritatif; **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** = dokumentasi first-party vendor/proyek (bukan standar, mengikat hanya untuk produk/perusahaan itu); **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** = rekomendasi praktik dari vendor/handbook, bukan norma standar; **[INFERENSI]** = turunan logis peneliti dari sumber bersitasi; **[OPINI]** = penilaian peneliti. Setiap klaim normatif di bawah selalu menyertakan nama dokumen + URL lengkap. Relevansi dibatasi praktik 2024–2026; versi standar disebut eksplisit. Catatan batas scope: catatan ini sengaja **tidak** membahas BRD, FSD, desain arsitektur/UI, test plan, struktur folder/repo, atau API versioning (topik peneliti 1–4 dan 6). Penyebutan path seperti `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` atau `.gitlab/issue_templates/` di bawah **hanya** merupakan lokasi konfigurasi template issue/MR yang termasuk objektif tooling topik ini, bukan rekomendasi konvensi struktur repositori.

Ringkasan versi sumber: **The 2020 Scrum Guide** (November 2020, masih merupakan definisi Scrum yang dipublikasikan di scrumguides.org saat diakses 2026-11-04) — **[STANDAR RESMI]** The 2020 Scrum Guide — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **ISO 21502:2020** (edisi 1, terbit 2020-12, status "International Standard to be revised [90.92]" per laman ISO, sehingga masih berlaku dengan revisi dalam proses) — **[STANDAR RESMI]** ISO, "ISO 21502:2020 Project, programme and portfolio management — Guidance on project management" — https://www.iso.org/standard/74947.html ; **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 / GDPR** — **[STANDAR RESMI]** EUR-Lex, "Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation)" — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng ; **DORA software delivery performance metrics** (dimutakhirkan 5 Januari 2026, model lima metrik) — **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** dora.dev, "DORA's software delivery performance metrics" — https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics-four-keys/ ; **GitLab Sync vs. Async Culture** (dimodifikasi 26 Oktober 2026) — **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** GitLab Handbook — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/ .

## Apa tujuan laporan harian, apa bedanya dengan daily Scrum/standup, weekly status report, dan burndown, serta kapan ia menjadi overhead yang merugikan?

### Takeaway
Laporan harian adalah artefak komunikasi proyek untuk audiens di luar tim (akuntabilitas, keputusan, risiko), sedangkan Daily Scrum adalah **event internal Developers untuk inspeksi–adaptasi**, bukan status report; mencampur keduanya adalah akar "status theater". **[STANDAR RESMI]** The 2020 Scrum Guide — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates

### Cited Findings
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** "The purpose of the Daily Scrum is to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog as necessary, adjusting the upcoming planned work"; pesertanya adalah "the Developers of the Scrum Team" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Daily Scrum" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** "Daily Scrums improve communications, identify impediments, promote quick decision-making, and consequently eliminate the need for other meetings"; Scrum juga menyatakan event dipakai "to minimize the need for meetings not defined in Scrum" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Daily Scrum" dan "Scrum Events" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Scrum Guide **tidak memerintahkan** format tiga pertanyaan; sebaliknya: "The Developers can select whatever structure and techniques they want, as long as their Daily Scrum focuses on progress toward the Sprint Goal and produces an actionable plan for the next day of work" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Daily Scrum" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Format tiga pertanyaan ("What did I work on yesterday? / What am I working on today? / What issues are blocking me?") adalah praktik Atlassian, bukan ketentuan Scrum Guide; Atlassian juga menegaskan stand-up "aren't a time to plan; Sprint planning is for planning" dan "aren't the only time to mention blockers. If you're stuck, ask for help!" — Atlassian, "What is a stand up meeting & tips to run one" — https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Sprint Review adalah event terpisah untuk "inspect the outcome of the Sprint" bersama stakeholders, dan "should avoid limiting it to a presentation" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Sprint Review" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Burndown disebut sebagai salah satu "practices ... to forecast progress, like burn-downs, burn-ups, or cumulative flows", dengan peringatan "these do not replace the importance of empiricism. In complex environments, what will happen is unknown. Only what has already happened may be used for forward-looking decision making" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "The Sprint" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** Burndown chart Jira menampilkan "kerja aktual versus estimasi yang harus diselesaikan dalam sprint", dengan sumbu x = waktu dan sumbu y = issue/kartu — Atlassian Jira Software Cloud, "Burndown Chart" — https://ja.confluence.atlassian.com/jirasoftwarecloud/burndown-chart-777002653.html ; rujukan fitur juga ada di Atlassian, "Use os relatórios do Jira para tomar decisões baseadas em dados" — https://www.atlassian.com/br/software/jira/features/reports
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Weekly project update didefinisikan sebagai "progress reports in writing or a video that share the current status, wins, and challenges", disarankan "Once a week at the end of the week", dengan tujuan "clear, accountable record of project progress" dan "decisions and rationale, risks, and recent learnings" — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Riset internal yang dikutip Atlassian menyatakan "70% of knowledge workers say meetings are ineffective for status updates"; ini adalah klaim riset vendor, bukan standar — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Contoh nyata organisasi remote justru memilih **weekly** async status update (bukan daily) untuk transparansi lintas tim: "We maintain a practice of weekly async status updates ... every Wednesday ... Updates are required for all assigned issues" — GitLab Handbook, "Weekly Async Updates" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/ai/ai-coding/how-we-work/async-updates/
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** ISO 21502:2020 memberi panduan manajemen proyek untuk organisasi apa pun dan "delivery approach can be ... predictive, incremental, iterative, adaptive or hybrid, including agile approaches", sehingga pelaporan proyek dan Scrum tidak saling meniadakan — **[STANDAR RESMI]** ISO/TC 258, "ISO 21502:2020" — https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc258/home/projects/published/iso-21502.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** ISO 21502:2020 memiliki klausul **7.15 Reporting** dengan sub-klausul 7.15.1 Overview, 7.15.2 Planning reporting, 7.15.3 Managing reporting, 7.15.4 Delivering reports, serta 7.16 Information and documentation management — Daftar Isi resmi yang dipublikasikan DIN untuk ISO 21502:2020(E) — https://www.din.de/en/getting-involved/standards-committees/nqsz/publications/wdc-beuth:din21:334145134/toc-3233385/download ; konfirmasi silang pada sampel BSI — https://www.normsplash.com/Samples/BSI/147331446/BS-ISO-21502-2020-en.pdf
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Aturan eksplisit kapan rapat berulang menjadi overhead: "Don't run a meeting to share information that could've been a doc"; "Don't let recurring meetings outlive their purpose. Kill them or shrink them every quarter"; "Don't treat showing up as your contribution. Prep is required to participate" — GitLab Handbook, "Sync vs. Async Culture" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Atlassian menganjurkan mengevaluasi efektivitas stand-up di retrospective: "If the team isn't finding value in a stand-up, discuss why. Make some changes!"; bahkan "Some Atlassian teams meet daily. Others meet three times a week" — Atlassian, "What is a stand up meeting & tips to run one" — https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups

### Inferences
- **[INFERENSI]** Matriks pembeda: **Daily Scrum** = sinkron/15 menit/audien Developers/tujuan adaptasi rencana hari ini; **daily status report** = tertulis/audien manajemen-klien/tujuan akuntabilitas + keputusan; **weekly status report** = agregasi mingguan + tren + risiko + lessons; **burndown** = visualisasi kuantitatif sisa kerja, bukan narasi keputusan. Dasar: **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates ; **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** https://ja.confluence.atlassian.com/jirasoftwarecloud/burndown-chart-777002653.html
- **[INFERENSI]** Laporan harian menjadi overhead merugikan ketika: (a) isinya bisa digantikan dokumen/board yang sudah ter-update otomatis; (b) tidak ada keputusan atau eskalasi yang dihasilkan; (c) dibuat duplikat untuk stand-up **dan** manajemen; (d) rapat berulang tidak pernah dievaluasi ulang. Dasar: **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/ ; **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups
- **[INFERENSI]** Untuk 2024–2026, pola yang paling hemat adalah: biarkan Daily Scrum tetap milik tim, dan kirim ringkasan **tertulis/asinkron** ke audiens eksternal hanya pada kedalaman yang mereka butuhkan (harian hanya jika ada keputusan/risiko; mingguan untuk tren). Dasar: **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/ai/ai-coding/how-we-work/async-updates/ ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates

### Gaps
- Tidak ditemukan standar resmi yang **melarang atau mewajibkan** frekuensi harian pelaporan ke klien; ISO 21502:2020 klausul 7.15 berbayar, sehingga isi normatif sub-klausul 7.15.1–7.15.4 tidak dapat diverifikasi dari teks gratis. Hanya daftar isi yang dikonfirmasi: https://www.iso.org/standard/74947.html dan https://www.din.de/en/getting-involved/standards-committees/nqsz/publications/wdc-beuth:din21:334145134/toc-3233385/download
- Angka "70% of knowledge workers" berasal dari riset internal Atlassian tanpa tautan ke metodologi/laporan lengkap di halaman yang diambil; perlakukan sebagai indikator arah, bukan bukti normatif: https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates
- Tidak ada sumber primer yang memberi ambang kuantitatif (mis. jumlah menit per orang atau jumlah penerima) untuk menyatakan "daily report = overhead"; keputusan ini bersifat konteks organisasi.

## Format "yesterday / today / blockers" dan aturan 15 menit menurut Scrum Guide resmi, serta versi asinkron/tertulis untuk tim remote dan lintas zona waktu?

### Takeaway
Aturan **15 menit** memang normatif dalam The 2020 Scrum Guide, tetapi **tiga pertanyaan yesterday/today/blockers bukan berasal dari Scrum Guide** — itu praktik Atlassian; Scrum Guide 2020 justru memberi kebebasan struktur. Versi asinkron yang relevan 2024–2026 adalah update tertulis dengan owner, "respond by", dan closing loop, bukan sekadar memindahkan round-robin ke chat. **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/

### Cited Findings
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** "The Daily Scrum is a 15-minute event for the Developers of the Scrum Team. To reduce complexity, it is held at the same time and place every working day of the Sprint" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Daily Scrum" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** "The Developers can select whatever structure and techniques they want" dengan syarat fokus pada Sprint Goal dan menghasilkan "an actionable plan for the next day of work"; "The Daily Scrum is not the only time Developers are allowed to adjust their plan" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Daily Scrum" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Scrum Master bertugas "Causing the removal of impediments to the Scrum Team's progress" dan "Ensuring that all Scrum events take place and are positive, productive, and kept within the timebox" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Scrum Master" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** "A stand up meeting is designed to be brief, typically lasting no more than 15 minutes"; tiga pertanyaan yang dipakai Atlassian: "What did I accomplish yesterday? / What will I do today? / Are there any blockers?" — Atlassian, "What is a stand up meeting & tips to run one" — https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Heuristik remote Atlassian: "If one team member is remote, treat every team member as remote"; untuk tim lintas zona waktu mereka memberi contoh stand-up pukul 15.30 waktu San Francisco agar sejajar dengan Sydney, serta opsi "Share written updates in Slack" dan "Record video updates with Loom" — Atlassian, "What is a stand up meeting & tips to run one" — https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** GitLab menetapkan default: "Default here for status, FYIs, decisions with clear context, proposals, and anything that benefits from being searchable later"; "Slack, docs, and GitLab issues are our async layer. They scale across timezones, create durable artifacts" — GitLab Handbook, "Sync vs. Async Culture" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Aturan asinkron yang bisa langsung dipakai: "Write the issue, doc, or thread as if the reader has none of your context"; "Tag decision owners explicitly and set a 'respond by' date so threads don't drift"; "Close the loop in writing once a decision is made, even if it was made in a meeting"; "Don't post a wall of text and expect engagement; if it's that long, it needs a TL;DR and a clear ask"; "Don't confuse 'I sent a message' with 'we aligned'" — GitLab Handbook, "Sync vs. Async Culture" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Pembagian medium: "Use documents for content that requires a narrative like proposals or memos. Use GitLab issues for work that has a lifecycle, and use Slack for ephemeral coordination" — GitLab Handbook, "Sync vs. Async Culture" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Kapan tetap sinkron: keputusan dengan ambiguitas, resolusi konflik, membangun kepercayaan, brainstorming; serta "Capture the decision and owners in writing within 24 hours, in the relevant issue or doc, so the sync output becomes async durable" — GitLab Handbook, "Sync vs. Async Culture" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Template async nyata GitLab (walau **mingguan**): "Progress & Status / Next Steps / Blockers / How confident are you that this will make it to the current milestone? [Not confident / Slightly confident / Very confident]", plus "_Remember to update the workflow label!_" dan "/cc" ke engineering manager, product manager, serta kolaborator; best practice: "Be specific and concise", "Always include next steps, even if they're tentative", "Flag blockers early", "Link to relevant issues or documentation" — GitLab Handbook, "Weekly Async Updates" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/ai/ai-coding/how-we-work/async-updates/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** GitLab juga mendokumentasikan agenda stand-up harian sebagai dokumen **yang terus dipakai (bukan dibuat baru tiap hari)**: "At GitLab, daily standups exist to quickly align and accelerate an initiative. They are scheduled for a limited duration ... less risk of important information being lost from week to week, as a net new agenda is not created", dengan tag aksi `TODO / DOTO / DISCUSS / REVIEW / HELP / DONE` dan tautan ke issue/MR — GitLab Handbook, "Suggested 1:1 Agenda and Daily Standup Format" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/leadership/1-1/suggested-agenda-format/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Atlassian menyarankan format mingguan tetap ringkas: "A tight headline and 1-3 sentences ... Tag them and provide clear direction on what you need and by when", dengan detail tambahan di bagian "Go deeper" / apendiks — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates

### Inferences
- **[INFERENSI]** Koreksi umum yang perlu ditegaskan kepada report writer: menyebut "aturan yesterday/today/blockers **menurut Scrum Guide**" adalah tidak tepat. 15 menit = Scrum Guide; tiga pertanyaan = Atlassian/industri. Dasar: **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups
- **[INFERENSI]** Untuk lintas zona waktu, "asinkron" bukan berarti tanpa batas waktu: perlu `{{batas_waktu_respons}}` + `{{owner_keputusan}}` + penutupan loop tertulis; tanpa itu terjadi "eventual consistency on decisions" yang disebut GitLab. Dasar: **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[INFERENSI]** Format tulisan yang aman adalah satu dokumen/thread per periode yang diperbarui (bukan pesan baru tiap hari), karena mengurangi copy-paste dan menyimpan jejak; ini selaras dengan agenda stand-up GitLab yang tidak dibuat ulang tiap hari. Dasar: **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/leadership/1-1/suggested-agenda-format/

### Gaps
- Scrum Guide tidak memberi panduan **asinkron** untuk Daily Scrum; tidak ada ketentuan resmi tentang pengganti tertulis bagi tim tanpa overlap jam kerja. Sumber: https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- Tidak ditemukan angka SLA respons harian yang ditetapkan standar resmi; satu-satunya angka first-party adalah "within 24 hours" untuk **mencatat keputusan** pasca-meeting sinkron, bukan SLA blocker: https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/

## Apa isi minimal laporan harian proyek (identitas, progress, deliverable, WIP, blocker, perubahan scope/jadwal, risiko, metrik, keputusan, rencana)?

### Takeaway
Isi minimal dapat dipetakan hampir seluruhnya ke sumber resmi: identitas/status dari GitHub Projects status updates, deliverable dari Definition of Done, perubahan scope dari aturan Sprint Goal, risiko/keputusan dari Atlassian weekly updates, serta blocker dari issue dependencies. **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/about-projects ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates

### Cited Findings
- **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** Identitas + status + periode: GitHub Projects status updates memungkinkan "set a status, such as 'On track' or 'At risk'", "set start dates and target dates", dan pesan ber-Markdown yang tampil di side panel/header — GitHub Docs, "About Projects" — https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/about-projects
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Ringkasan progress harus bersandar pada Sprint Backlog: "a highly visible, real-time picture of the work that the Developers plan to accomplish ... updated throughout the Sprint as more is learned. It should have enough detail that they can inspect their progress in the Daily Scrum" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Sprint Backlog" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Deliverable "selesai" hanya boleh diklaim bila memenuhi Definition of Done: "The moment a Product Backlog item meets the Definition of Done, an Increment is born"; "If a Product Backlog item does not meet the Definition of Done, it cannot be released or even presented at the Sprint Review" — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "Increment" dan "Commitment: Definition of Done" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Perubahan scope/jadwal: "No changes are made that would endanger the Sprint Goal"; "Scope may be clarified and renegotiated with the Product Owner as more is learned"; pembatalan Sprint hanya otoritas Product Owner — The 2020 Scrum Guide, bab "The Sprint" — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** Pekerjaan berjalan + blocker + owner: GitHub menyarankan "Assign collaborators to issues to communicate responsibility", "@mentions to alert a person or entire team", "clearly define which issues are blocked by, or blocking, other issues", serta mendukung sub-issues dan issue dependencies — GitHub Docs, "Best practices for Projects" — https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/best-practices-for-projects
- **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** Target selesai blocker/ETA: custom fields mencakup "A date field to track target ship dates", "A single select field to track whether a task is Low, Medium, or High priority", dan iteration field — GitHub Docs, "About Projects" dan "Best practices for Projects" — https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/about-projects ; https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/best-practices-for-projects
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Risiko + keputusan + alasan: "Record key project decisions, along with their rationale"; "If there's a potential risk – big or small – that could impact project success, document it"; pertanyaan panduan mencakup "What could cause this project to miss its success criteria?" dan "Has new information surfaced that makes the timeline or execution more challenging than expected?" — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Perubahan jadwal harus disertai sebab: "If deadlines need adjusting, edit them and say why" — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Keputusan yang dibutuhkan: "If you need feedback from stakeholders, tag them and provide clear direction on what you need and by when"; GitLab: "Tag decision owners explicitly and set a 'respond by' date" — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates ; GitLab Handbook, "Sync vs. Async Culture" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Rencana periode berikutnya: "Focus: What's next" / "Always include next steps, even if they're tentative" — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates ; GitLab Handbook, "Weekly Async Updates" — https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/ai/ai-coding/how-we-work/async-updates/
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Metrik/defect/ETA sebagai bagian dari pelaporan proyek memiliki landasan klausul **7.15 Reporting** (7.15.1–7.15.4) dan **7.16 Information and documentation management** dalam ISO 21502:2020 — Daftar Isi resmi ISO 21502:2020(E) — https://www.din.de/en/getting-involved/standards-committees/nqsz/publications/wdc-beuth:din21:334145134/toc-3233385/download
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Isi minimum versi ringkas Atlassian: "Wins: What was accomplished this week / Needs Input: Any blockers or questions / Focus: What's next", ditambah decisions, risks, learnings — Atlassian Team Playbook, "Weekly Project Updates" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates

### Inferences
- **[INFERENSI]** Daftar isi minimal yang disarankan (sintesis, bukan kutipan standar): `{{id_proyek}}`, `{{periode}}`, `{{status_RAG}}`, ringkasan 1–3 kalimat, deliverable `Done` + evidence ID, WIP + issue ID, blocker + owner + target + level, delta scope/jadwal + alasan, risiko + mitigasi, metrik (burndown/velocity/ETA/defect) + sumber data, keputusan yang diminta + owner + `{{batas_waktu}}`, rencana hari berikutnya, serta tautan arsip. Dasar: **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/about-projects ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates
- **[INFERENSI]** Aturan praktis: setiap baris klaim progress wajib memiliki **pointer ke artefak** (issue/PR/MR/CI run), sehingga "deliverable selesai" dapat diuji terhadap Definition of Done. Dasar: **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/best-practices-for-projects
- **[INFERENSI]** Jika tidak ada perubahan sejak laporan sebelumnya, baris yang boleh diisi adalah "tidak ada perubahan" + tautan ke laporan sebelumnya, bukan mengulang narasi; ini menekan copy-paste. ## Metrik apa yang boleh dipakai, bagaimana menghindari "status theater" dan progres persentase subyektif, serta peran metrik DORA/flow dan evidence PR/issue ID?

## Template utama siap pakai, Checklist review, dan Anti-pattern yang sering terjadi

### Takeaway
Bagian ini menyatukan seluruh temuan menjadi satu artefak operasional: template Markdown lengkap ber-placeholder, daftar periksa yang dapat dipakai reviewer, dan daftar anti-pattern yang masing-masing diberi penangkal bersitasi. **[STANDAR RESMI]** https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates ; **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/ai/ai-coding/how-we-work/async-updates/

### Cited Findings
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Template dasar yang dijadikan acuan: "Wins / Needs Input / Focus" (Atlassian) dan "Progress & Status / Next Steps / Blockers / confidence" (GitLab) — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates ; https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/ai/ai-coding/how-we-work/async-updates/
- **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** Status RAG dan target date yang dipakai template di bawah tersedia sebagai fitur resmi: "On track"/"At risk", "start dates and target dates" — GitHub Docs, "About Projects" — https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects/learning-about-projects/about-projects
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Aturan keputusan + batas waktu: "tag them and provide clear direction on what you need and by when" dan "set a 'respond by' date" — https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/weekly-project-updates ; https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/sync-vs-async-culture/
- **[STANDAR RESMI]** Kriteria `Done` pada template mengikuti Definition of Done — https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **[DOKUMENTASI RESMI]** Anti-gaming metrik mengikuti daftar "Common pitfalls" DORA — https://dora.dev/guides/dora-metrics-four-keys/
- **[PRAKTIK INDUSTRI]** Anti-surveillance mengikuti "They aren't about inflating egos or justifying job descriptions" dan "1:1s are private meetings" — https://www.atlassian.com/agile/scrum/standups ; https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/leadership/1-1/suggested-agenda-format/

### Anti-pattern yang sering terjadi
Tiap baris: gejala → mengapa merugikan → penangkal bersitasi.

- **[OPINI]** Template di atas sengaja memadukan kedalaman harian (blocker/keputusan/rencana) dengan disiplin laporan mingguan (risiko/metrik/learnings); untuk proyek tanpa keputusan harian, bagian 5–7 boleh diringkas menjadi satu baris "tidak ada perubahan" agar tidak berubah jadi overhead.
